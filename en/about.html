About

Hi, I'm Rishi. I build websites for people like you.

I'm an experienced software engineer (25+ years). I have developed and maintained many web applications, and have also built websites on various platforms. I use that knowledge to build websites that are fast and secure, fully custom-made for you or your business, and to do it without the long waiting times and high prices you may know from agencies.

Many small businesses and freelancers just want a good website: one that looks professional, tells people what they do, and brings in customers. They don't want to hear about hosting, plugins or code, and they shouldn't have to.

That's why I keep it simple. You talk to me, not to a team of strangers. You tell me about your business, I do the technical work, and you approve the result before you pay.

I use the most modern tools and techniques, including AI. Yes, the bigger agencies and studios do that too, but they still make you pay sometimes twice as much, or even much more, for a website. I have no overhead costs, which is why I can keep the price friendly and low.

What you can expect

  • Personal contact: one person, from first call to launch
  • Plain language, in Dutch (or English if you prefer), no technical jargon
  • Fixed prices, agreed before we start
  • Two design options to choose from
  • Fast, secure, search-friendly websites
  • You pay only after your approval
