Questions

Good to know

How much does a website cost?

A Basic website (3 pages) is €400 and a Standard website (5 pages) is €750. As an introductory offer you now get 10% off (Basic €360, Standard €675). Need more, like a bigger site, a booking page or something special? Tell me what you want and I give you a fixed quote.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

None. I take care of everything technical. You only tell me about your business, send me what you have (texts, logo, photos, examples) and say what you like or dislike.

Can I edit the website myself?

Not by default, and that is a deliberate choice: your website stays static, so it stays fast and secure. For the first 3 months, content changes by me are included. You can also choose an optional subscription: make changes yourself, or have me make them. All details and prices are on the page Services & prices.

What do I need from you?

Everything you already have: your logo, photos, texts, a list of services, opening hours, links to your social media and to websites you like. Messy is fine. I'll sort it out. Don't have a text yet? We'll talk about it on the phone.

What if I don't like the design?

You get two different design options and we look at them together in a short meeting, online or on location. You tell me what you would like to have changed, and the next version is hopefully the one you sign off. One small change more or less is no problem.

When do I pay?

After you have signed off the final version in a secure environment you receive the invoice. When it is paid, your website goes online. No payment before you have seen and approved the result.

Will people find my website on Google?

Every site I build has a strong search foundation: fast loading, mobile friendly, clear page titles and descriptions, a clean structure and the right technical set-up. Nobody can honestly promise a #1 position, but you start with the best possible basis.
