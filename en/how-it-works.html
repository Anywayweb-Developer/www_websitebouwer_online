Way of working

Clear steps, no surprises

You always know what happens next and what you need to do to get the best result, quickly. I take you along and help you towards the end result you want.

  1. You send me a messageJust a few lines about you and what you need. Takes two minutes.
    You: 2 minutes
  2. I call you or I visit youA relaxed conversation, remote or on location, of max. 30 minutes. I ask about everything I need to build a website that fits you and works towards your goals.
    Max. 30 minutes
  3. You send me your materialLogo, photos, texts, ideas, links to websites you like. Whatever you have, messy is fine.
    You: send what you have
  4. I build two design optionsTwo different, fully custom directions, so you can see what works for you instead of imagining it.
    Me: I do the work
  5. Short meeting, online or on locationTogether we look at both options in a secure environment. You react, I listen.
    Short meeting
  6. You tell me what to changeChoose your favourite, say what should be different. I process it.
    You: your feedback
  7. Final version for sign-offYou receive the next version in a secure environment to approve. Small changes are still possible at this point.
    For approval
  8. You pay the invoiceOnly after you have approved the result.
    Invoice
  9. Your website goes onlineI put everything live. From now on customers can find you. I also hand over the documentation and manuals/instructions. Prefer a personal walkthrough? Then we'll go through everything together in an online meeting.
    Live!
Tips for a fast result

How to get the best website, fast

  • Send everything in one go. Fewer rounds, faster result.
  • Tell me who your ideal customer or audience is. The site is built around them.
  • Show me examples. Websites you like say more than a thousand words.
  • Give your feedback in one list after the meeting.
  • Approve with care. After sign-off only small changes are possible. But don't worry. We'll work it out.
Questions

Good to know

Can I edit the website myself?

Not by default, and that is a deliberate choice: your website stays static, so it stays fast and secure. For the first 3 months, content changes by me are included. You can also choose an optional subscription: make changes yourself, or have me make them. All details and prices are on the page Services & prices.

What do I need from you?

Everything you already have: your logo, photos, texts, a list of services, opening hours, links to your social media and to websites you like. Messy is fine. I'll sort it out. Don't have a text yet? We'll talk about it on the phone.

What if I don't like the design?

You get two different design options and we look at them together in a short meeting, online or on location. You tell me what you would like to have changed, and the next version is hopefully the one you sign off. One small change more or less is no problem.

When do I pay?

After you have signed off the final version in a secure environment you receive the invoice. When it is paid, your website goes online. No payment before you have seen and approved the result.
